# Questions for Charmaine — document conflicts and missing decisions

Compiled 2026-09-10 from a page-by-page fidelity audit of the website build
against the handover package. The build follows the handover documents exactly
where they are clear; the items below are where the documents contradict each
other or where a decision is needed before we can proceed faithfully.

## Document conflicts (need Charmaine's call)

1. **Doghouse Deli — two versions (X vs Y).** The package contains two Deli
   documents with different wording and different treat lists:
   "X DOGHOUSE DELI.docx" and "Y DOGHOUSE DELI with Deli Information.docx".
   Y states it "contains all information currently approved for Doghouse Deli",
   so the site currently follows Y throughout (including its treat names, e.g.
   "Shark Crispies", and its rule not to publicly promise subscription
   pause/skip functionality yet). Please confirm Y is the version to use.

2. **Master Waitlist — two form specs (Q vs ZZZB).** "Q PUPPIES Master
   Waitlist.docx" and "ZZZB MASTER WAITLIST FORM.docx" differ on fields and on
   the success wording. Which document should the form follow?

3. **Guardian Home application — two specs (G vs ZZZC).** "G ABOUT US Guardian
   Homes.docx" and "ZZZC GUARDIAN HOME APPLICATION FORM.docx" differ on form
   length and on whether the application sits on the same page or a separate
   page. The site currently uses the ZZZC form on a separate page. Which is
   correct?

4. **Naming — "Guardian Program" vs "Guardian Homes".** "B ABOUT US General
   Information.docx" says "Guardian Program"; "G ABOUT US Guardian Homes.docx"
   says "Guardian Homes". Which name should the site use?

5. **Contact form dropdown — two option lists (ZZZA vs Z).** "ZZZA CONTACT US
   FORM.docx" lists eight enquiry topics; "Z RESOURCES Section.docx" page 6
   describes a different form (first/last name, suburb, state; options
   "Website or general enquiry" / "Other"). The site currently follows ZZZA.
   Which is correct?

6. **Missing approved page builds.** "ZZZL" references approved builds of the
   Current Litters, The Doghouse Method™ and First Eight Weeks pages, but
   those builds were not in the original file set. On 2026-09-10 Charmaine
   supplied the two missing builds: "XXXX_The_Doghouse_Method.docx" and
   "XXXXX_The_first_8_weeks.docx" (both now built). The approved Current
   Litters build is still outstanding — the site still shows an honest
   holding page with no invented content. Could you please send the
   approved Current Litters build or copy?
