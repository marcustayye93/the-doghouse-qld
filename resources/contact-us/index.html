Contact Us

We'd love to hear from you

Whether you're considering welcoming a Doghouse puppy into your family, have a question about our breeding program, would like to know more about a Guardian opportunity or simply can't find the information you're looking for, you're welcome to get in touch.

The Doghouse QLD is located in Caboolture, Queensland, north of Brisbane.

As a working breeding home rather than a retail premises, we aren't open for drop-in visits. Visits are arranged by appointment where appropriate as part of our puppy or Guardian Home process.

Before sending an enquiry, you may find that your question has already been answered in our Frequently Asked Questions.

Otherwise, send us a message below and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Existing Doghouse families

Already part of our Doghouse family?

Please don't feel that you need to use the website to find every answer.

If one of our puppies already shares your home and you have a question or something isn't going quite as expected, you're always welcome to contact us.

Sometimes it's a quick question about feeding, toilet training, grooming or behaviour.

Sometimes you simply want a little reassurance.

And sometimes you just want to send us a photograph and show us what one of our babies has grown into.

We love those messages too.

Where something requires veterinary diagnosis or treatment, we'll always encourage you to contact your veterinarian, but we're very happy to continue being part of the support network around the puppies we've raised.

A little note before visiting

The Doghouse QLD is our home as well as the place where we raise our puppies.

For the safety and wellbeing of our dogs, puppies and family, we don't offer unscheduled visits or general puppy viewing.

Visits are arranged with prospective families at the appropriate stage of our puppy or Guardian Home process.

Thank you for understanding and respecting our home.
